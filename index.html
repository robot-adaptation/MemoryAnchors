01 · About

About

This is a minimal starting point that borrows the layout, type, and color system of the reference site — a sticky top nav, an on-page outline rail, a centered title block, and calm prose columns — without any of its research content.

Swap this copy for your own project description. The page ships in the light “day” theme by default, with a full dark theme one click away via the toggle in the top-right corner.
